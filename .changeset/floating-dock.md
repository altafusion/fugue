Add `FloatingDock`: a bar fixed to the bottom of the page that the user drags by its grip (or moves with the arrow keys), that remembers its position, and whose button opens a panel above it. Set `storage-key` and `label`.

`FloatingDock` also takes `align` (which end of its box the bar sits at) and `margin` (distance from the page edges), so a dock can line up with something beside it.
