Make `DevToolsPanel` self-contained: mount `<DevToolsPanel />` once and it owns its open state, shows a draggable dock, and opens the drawer on `?qetools` (the new default `openParams`; `?qetools=off` turns it off). Sections now register through `useDevTools().registerSection()` or `nuxtApp.$fugueDevtools`, each `public` or `privileged`, with a reactive `setAccessResolver`. `@altafusion/fugue-content` registers its Studio drafts as a privileged section when fugue-devtools is installed.
